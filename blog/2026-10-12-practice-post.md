   ## Opening the courthouse doors

   Court records are public and available online, but finding them often costs a lot more money and time than it should. That's why **Free Law Project** makes them searchable for everyone.

   - Journalists can track cases that they are writing stories on
   - Researchers can study the courts to see how they have changed or progressed over the years
   - Anyone can read the law that governs them, even your grandmother

   Learn more about the free law library we've built at [free.law](https://free.law).
